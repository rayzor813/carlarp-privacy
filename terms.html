The short version

CarLarp is a game about photographing cars you see. You own your photographs. You are responsible for what you post. Be decent to other players. If you are not, your account goes.

Zero tolerance

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Do not post anything hateful, harassing, sexual, violent, illegal, or designed to identify or target a person. Do not post photographs taken on private property you had no right to be on.

Every catch can be reported, and any player can be blocked, from their profile. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. A catch reported by three different players is hidden immediately, before anyone reviews it. Accounts that post objectionable content are removed.

Your photographs

You keep ownership of every photograph you take. By posting one you give CarLarp permission to show it inside the app — on the feed, the boards and your garage — and to generate artwork from it when you ask for that. Nothing is sold, licensed on, or used to advertise.

Post only photographs you took. If you believe something here infringes your copyright, write to the address below and it will be removed.

Generated artwork

Rendered car art is generated by AI from your photograph. It is an illustration, not a photograph, and it will not always be an exact likeness. Every rendered image carries metadata recording that it was generated by AI, which travels with the file if you save or share it.

Paying for things

Rendering artwork from a photograph costs a render. Everyone gets some free each month; you can buy more, and CarLarp+ includes more.

CarLarp+ is a monthly subscription. It renews automatically at the price shown when you buy it, and keeps renewing until you cancel. Cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions at least a day before it renews — cancelling stops the next renewal and does not shorten the month you have already paid for. Prices are shown before you buy and can change; a change never applies to a period already paid for.

Apple takes the money, not us. Purchases are handled by the App Store and charged to your Apple account, and Apple's refund policy is the one that applies. We never see your card. If Apple refunds a purchase, the thing it bought is withdrawn — a refunded subscription ends, and refunded renders are removed from your balance.

A render that has been spent has been delivered, whether or not you liked the picture. Unspent renders stay in your balance and do not expire while your account exists; they are not money, cannot be transferred, and have no cash value.

If we stop selling something, you keep what you have already paid for until the period you paid for ends.

Identification is a guess

CarLarp identifies cars automatically and is sometimes wrong. Nothing here is a valuation, an appraisal, or advice of any kind, and the values shown are for play. Do not rely on them for anything that matters.

Deleting your account

Settings → Account → Delete account removes your profile, catches, scores and published photographs from our servers, and revokes the Sign in with Apple credential. The private photo backup in your own iCloud belongs to your Apple account and we cannot reach it — delete it from iCloud settings, or by deleting the app.
